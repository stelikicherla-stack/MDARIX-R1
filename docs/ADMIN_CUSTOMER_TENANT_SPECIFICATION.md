# Customer and Tenant Administration Specification

The customer onboarding wizard contains Organization, Tenant, Plan &
Subscription, Limits, Security/Region, First Customer Administrator, Review,
and Provision steps. Required fields include company/legal name, tenant code,
country/region, data residency, environment, plan, subscription dates, user,
admin, connector, role, storage, and AI limits.

Customer 360 contains Overview, Subscription, Users, Customer Administrators,
Entitlements, Integrations, Mappings, Security, Usage, and Administrative
Audit. It deliberately excludes regulated customer business data.
