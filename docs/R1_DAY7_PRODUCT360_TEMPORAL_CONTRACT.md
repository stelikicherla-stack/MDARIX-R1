# MDARIX R1 Day 7 Product 360 + Temporal Contract

## Day 7 Inputs

Day 7 may consume:

- Reality Graph node lookup
- Product graph
- Investigation graph
- Bounded neighborhoods
- Bounded paths
- Relationship detail/provenance
- Evidence summaries
- Canonical product/product-version/component/supplier/lot/change/complaint/risk/evidence data
- Temporal relationship metadata
- Quality limitations and unresolved gaps

## Day 7 Scope

Day 7 will build Product 360, lifecycle timeline, and temporal reality views.

## Day 7 Must Preserve

- Graph path does not mean causality.
- Missing relationships remain visible as gaps.
- Source provenance remains inspectable.
- Historical product versions remain distinct.
- Ground Truth remains evaluation-only.

## Not Day 6

Day 6 does not implement UI, timeline visualization, or Product 360 screens.
