# Stage 3 Reporting Architecture

Reports are interactive first and exportable only according to authorization. The catalog includes Product Intelligence, ProductVersion Impact, Complaint Trend, Signal Assessment, Investigation, Evidence & Provenance, Risk Impact, Scenario Comparison, Decision Brief, AI Assurance, Audit & Approval, and Executive Quality Review. JSON, CSV, and PDF are the initial formats; report generation must preserve Case Context, provenance, temporal scope, tenant, and human-review state.
