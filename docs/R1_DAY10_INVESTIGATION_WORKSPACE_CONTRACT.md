- Canonical Investigation context

- Reality Graph context

- EvidenceChunks
- EvidenceObservations
- Source anchors
- Trusted retrieval service
- Structured retrieval
- Semantic retrieval
- Temporal retrieval

- No-match and insufficient-evidence behavior

## Day 10 Scope

Day 10 will build the Investigation Workspace and deterministic investigation context presentation.

## Guardrail
