# MDARIX R1 UI Information Architecture

## Status

PROPOSED for Day 1 architecture freeze.

## UI Principle

Clean, focused, investigation-centric, evidence-first.

Avoid generic analytics dashboards, decorative widgets, and unnecessary navigation.

## Primary Screens

1. Login
2. Home / Decision Center
3. Investigation Workspace
4. Lifecycle Timeline
5. Reality Graph
6. Evidence Panel
7. Hypothesis Panel
8. AI Challenger
9. Unknowns Radar
10. Failure Chain
11. Counterfactual Analysis
12. Investigation / Decision Brief

## Administrative Architecture

- Users
- Roles
- Data sources
- Connector configuration
- AI configuration
- Templates
- Permissions
- Audit
- Tenant settings

## Decision Center

Decision Center surfaces investigation question, current state, timeline, leading hypotheses, competing hypotheses, supporting evidence, contradictions, unknowns, failure chain, constrained scenario results, AI recommendations, human decisions, and evidence/provenance links.

## Investigation Brief Sections

Investigation Question, Product Context, Executive Investigation Summary, Reconstructed Timeline, Key Changes, Relevant Evidence, Competing Hypotheses, Supporting Evidence, Contradicting Evidence, Unknowns, Failure Chain, Counterfactual/Scenario Analysis, AI Challenger Findings, Investigation Quality Findings, Recommended Next Evidence/Actions, Human Decision, Provenance/Audit References.
