# ADR-001: System of Intelligence vs System of Record

## Context
MDARIX connects lifecycle data from QMS, PLM, ERP, MES, and related systems.
## Decision
MDARIX R1 is a system of intelligence, not a system of record.
## Rationale
R1 must reconstruct and reason over product reality without replacing regulated source systems.
## Consequences
Source provenance, audit, evidence links, and data-quality status are mandatory.
## Alternatives Considered
Building a new QMS or PLM was rejected as out of R1 scope.
## Status
PROPOSED
