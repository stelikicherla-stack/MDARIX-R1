# ADR-006: Human Authority over Material Decisions

## Context
MDARIX supports regulated medical-device investigation and decision workflows.
## Decision
AI recommends; authorized humans decide.
## Rationale
Material regulated decisions require accountable human authority.
## Consequences
Decision records, human reviews, and approval states are first-class architecture requirements.
## Alternatives Considered
Autonomous regulatory, safety, CAPA, recall, product release, and closure decisions were rejected.
## Status
PROPOSED
