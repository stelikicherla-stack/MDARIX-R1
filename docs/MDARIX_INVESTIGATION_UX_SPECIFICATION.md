# Investigation UX Specification

An investigation view explains why it opened, its ProductVersion and signal scope, complaints, lots, changes, evidence, unknowns, and review state. Tabs are Context, Complaint Scope, Affected Scope, Product Changes, Evidence, Analysis, Hypotheses, Challenger, Unknowns, Failure Chain, Scenarios, and Investigation Brief.

Actions are read-only analysis unless a separately authorized human workflow is invoked. Every analysis result includes temporal scope, provenance, limitations, and human-review state.
