# MDARIX Navigation Information Architecture

Authenticated business navigation is organized as Home/Command Center, Product 360, Investigations, Evidence, Decision Center, AI Assurance, and Audit Trail. Administration is permission-gated and separated from customer business data. The existing shell preserves the connected workflow while the application is incrementally decomposed into feature modules.

Target additions are Story View, Signals & Complaints, Ask MDARIX, and Reports & Insights, backed by the Stage 3 contracts. No second authorization or tenant-resolution path is introduced.
